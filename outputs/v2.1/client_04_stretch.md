## Background & Objectives

We understand that, following the sale of James’s business, you want to build a tax-efficient investment base for long-term growth and to help replace his income when he steps back from full-time work. This has been a busy and emotionally significant transition for you both, and you want to put the available sale proceeds to work after repaying the bridging loan, without relying on the uncertain deferred earnout. You have no current need for portfolio income and want an approach consistent with your agreed balanced-to-moderate attitude to risk, while ensuring pension contributions remain within the applicable limits.
