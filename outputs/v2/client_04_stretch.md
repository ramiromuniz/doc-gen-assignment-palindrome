## Recommendations

- We recommend repaying James’s £200,000 bridging loan from the business-sale completion proceeds, confirming the repayment date before investing. This meets the existing commitment before allocating money towards your long-term objectives.

- We recommend topping up James’s Holloway Stocks & Shares ISA, H4-ISA-J, subject to confirming his available ISA allowance and the top-up amount. This supports long-term growth within a tax-efficient account.

- We recommend topping up Caroline’s Holloway Stocks & Shares ISA, H4-ISA-C, subject to confirming her available ISA allowance and the top-up amount. This also helps build a tax-efficient base for your future income needs.
