## Recommendations

We recommend the following:

- We recommend disinvesting the full value of the joint General Investment Account, currently valued at £40,000 as of 15 March 2026. This action will simplify your investment structure as you move into retirement, aligning with your preference to streamline your accounts.

- The proceeds from the sale of the joint GIA will be used to top up both of your Stocks & Shares ISAs. This will involve an equal split of the proceeds, with £20,000 allocated to each ISA.

- David's Stocks & Shares ISA, which has a current value of £61,000 as of 30 April 2026, will receive the £20,000 top-up, increasing its total value to £81,000.

- Susan's Stocks & Shares ISA, which has a current value of £58,500 as of 30 April 2026, will also receive the £20,000 top-up, increasing its total value to £78,500.

- Topping up your ISAs maximises your tax-efficient savings for the new tax year and leverages the full allowance available to both of you, ensuring you remain on track with your financial objectives.
