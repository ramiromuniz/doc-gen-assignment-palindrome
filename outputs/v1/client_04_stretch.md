## Recommendations

We recommend the following:

- We recommend using the full ISA allowances for the new tax year. This will involve a total investment of £20,000, with £10,000 contributed to your Stocks & Shares ISA on the Holloway platform and £10,000 contributed to Caroline's Stocks & Shares ISA, both accounted for in the ISAs.

- We suggest making pension contributions into your SIPPs, investing £40,000 each for you and Caroline into your respective SIPPs on the Brightwell platform. This aligns with your objective of building a tax-efficient base for future income.

- We recommend adding £45,000 to the jointly-held General Investment Account on the Holloway platform. This amount will be sourced from the proceeds of your recent business sale, as discussed during our meeting on 20 May 2026.

- We will also establish a new jointly-held investment account using the remaining balance from the business proceeds after the contributions have been made. Given the overall investment strategy, this will allow you to have more flexibility for future investments.

- It is essential to note that £200,000 of the £850,000 business sale proceeds is already committed to repaying a bridging loan, due shortly. Therefore, we will only be investing the available funds after this commitment is settled.

- We should also proceed with the partial rebalance of the Holloway joint GIA, as agreed in our meeting. This will involve liquidating a portion of the existing investments to facilitate a diversified approach, taking into consideration potential capital gains tax implications which we will outline later.

- Finally, we need to confirm the ongoing charges associated with these platforms and advise you of the total ongoing cost across the three platforms as soon as we have verified the figures. An initial charge of 0.5% will apply to these actions.
